Player controls Ryo and moves around in a maze

Mobs and friendlies spawn in at random and moves in x and y axis

If Ryo approaches friendlies, a message appears and
Bocchi +10 dollars
Kita +20 dollars

If Ryo approaches a mob, a message appears and
Nijika -All dollars
Kikuri - 10 dollars

Player must keep avoiding mobs and gain money from friendlies

Player can also collect money from random coins that appear around the map

Once the player collects a set amount of money, game ends.
